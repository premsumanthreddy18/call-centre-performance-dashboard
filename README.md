# call-centre-performance-dashboard
## Business Problem
A call centre operating across 3 cities (Cincinnati, Cleveland, 
and Columbus) needed a unified view of agent performance, call 
volume trends, and revenue metrics to identify operational 
inefficiencies and optimise representative productivity.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Data Model, Power Query, 
Slicers, Conditional Formatting

## Dataset
Call centre operations dataset containing 1,000 calls across 
5 representatives, 3 cities, and 12 months.

## Key KPIs Tracked
- Total Calls: 1,000
- Total Revenue: ₹96,623
- Total Call Duration: 89,850 minutes
- Average Customer Rating: 3.9
- Happy Callers (5-star rating): 307
- Monthly Call Trend (Jan–Dec)
- Weekly Call Trend (Mon–Sun)
- Calls and Revenue by Representative (R01–R05)
- Gender Split of Callers by City
- Customer Rating Distribution (1–5 scale)
- Revenue Matrix by City and Representative

## Key Insights
- Wednesday recorded the highest call volume at 38 calls — 
  indicating mid-week as peak operational demand
- R03 representative handled 218 calls with ₹20.6K revenue — 
  highest among all representatives
- Cincinnati generated the highest total revenue across all 
  3 cities combined
- October recorded the highest monthly call spike — suggesting 
  seasonal demand patterns worth investigating
- Female callers slightly outnumber male callers across all 
  3 cities — relevant for targeted communication strategy

## Dashboard Features
- Dynamic slicer connected to all visuals enabling 
  representative-level drill-down across all KPIs
- Monthly and weekly trend charts showing call volume patterns
- Clustered bar chart comparing male vs female caller 
  distribution across Cincinnati, Cleveland, and Columbus
- Revenue matrix table with conditional formatting showing 
  city-wise and representative-wise breakdown
- KPI cards with variance indicators for quick performance
